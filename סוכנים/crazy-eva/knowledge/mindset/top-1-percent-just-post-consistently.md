# Top 1% Secret: Just Post Consistently

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DW1GftaDXN_/
**Category**: mindset
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
The top 1% of creators don't have the cleverest strategy, they just have the most posts and have been publishing every single day for a long time without fail.

## How It Works
- All the biggest creators on social media share one trait: the most posts on their account
- They post every day, consistently, for years, never breaking the schedule
- Over time, competitors who started alongside you stop posting or leave the industry
- You don't beat them with strategy, you outlast them with consistency
- Authenticity amplifies this: creating from genuine expertise makes people like you even more

## Example
Eva started her social media niche account in 2023 from zero, in a space already dominated by established creators. Three years later of daily posting, many of those "top dogs" stopped, and she passed them.

## When to Use
When feeling discouraged by slow growth or questioning whether to keep going. The answer is almost always: keep posting. Consistency is unglamorous but it's the actual mechanism that works.
