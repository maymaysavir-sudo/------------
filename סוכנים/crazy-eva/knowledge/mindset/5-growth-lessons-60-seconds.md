# 5 Social Media Growth Lessons in 60 Seconds

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DUvT6SwEjxD/
**Category**: mindset
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
Five hard-won lessons from 5 years of social media growth, distilled into the principles that actually separate long-term winners from everyone else.

## How It Works
1. **Consistency beats everyone**, post on a schedule, never break it, never take long breaks. You will literally outlast and pass creators who started with more followers than you.
2. **You only get one reputation**, if your gut says don't post it, don't post it. Your reputation is worth more than one missed posting day. Repost an old video instead.
3. **Post all 3 content types**, TOFU + MOFU + BOFU together = personal brand with consistent views. Only posting one type = following with inconsistent views.
4. **Use undetectable viral structures, not just viral hooks**, find the underlying script architecture of viral videos, templatize it, write your scripts from it.
5. **Build content systems for your busy life**, you will always be busy. Build the systems that let you create consistently, or accept that you won't grow. There's no middle ground.

## When to Use
When onboarding a new client or creator who wants a "what really matters" overview. Also useful for a reality check when someone is stuck on tactics instead of fundamentals.
