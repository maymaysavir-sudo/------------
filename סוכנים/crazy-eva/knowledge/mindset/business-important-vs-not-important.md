# Growing a Business - Important vs. Not Important

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DTSqHbxERw1/?igsh=MTBuMzkyMmFwdW5iYw==
**Category**: mindset
**Added**: 2026-04-19

## Core Insight
Most new business owners waste energy on optics (logo, website, business cards) instead of the fundamentals that actually drive revenue. Clear offer, landing page, marketing, sales, and systems are what actually matter.

## How It Works
| Element | Rating | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Logo and brand colors | Not important | Eva didn't create hers until 1.5 years in |
| Clear offer | Important | No offer = no sales; bad offer = no sales |
| Fancy website | Not important | Eva barely uses hers |
| Landing page (offer + social proof + CTA) | Very important | The only sales tool she's ever needed |
| Outbound and inbound marketing | Important | Spreads awareness to the offer |
| Business cards | Not important | She doesn't have any |
| Sales | Important | No sales = no money |
| SOPs, systems, processes | Very very important | Required to scale beyond one person |
| Hiring and delegating | Important | You can only go so far alone |

## Example
Eva built her business to 1.5+ years without a logo or brand colors. She does have a landing page that functions as her website.

## When to Use
When prioritizing what to build first, or when feeling behind because of missing "business-looking" elements.
