# Storytelling Templates - Core Structures

**Source**: PBL (Personal Brand Launch) by Ava Yuergens, "Storytelling Structures" DOCX (also: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbiXUvEBKF0/ - adds The Challenge and The Big Goal/Dream structures)
**Category**: copywriting
**Added**: 2026-05-05 (updated 2026-08-03)

## Core Insight
Three fill-in-the-blank story structures that work across short and long-form content. Each structure has a defined arc, use the templates to build the script, then layer in your specific details.

## How It Works

---

### 1. HERO'S JOURNEY
Best for: client transformation stories, case studies, founder story

1. **INTRO**, Establish the hero and the problem
   > *"X days/years ago I [problem client was experiencing]"*

2. **INFLECTION POINT**, The pain this problem caused
   > *"Because of this I was [pain points/symptoms from the problem]"*

3. **RISING ACTION**, Failed solutions they tried
   > *"I tried/they tried [failed solutions]"*

4. **CLIMAX**, The breakthrough solution
   > *"Finally after X months/years of no results I figured out the one thing that actually worked: [solution]"*

5. **FALLING ACTION**, Results from the solution
   > *"When I started doing this I [results/social proof/success]"*

6. **RESOLUTION**, CTA to your offer
   > *"Because of this experience I started [business/service] to help people like me achieve [dream result]"*

---

### 2. MAN IN A HOLE
Best for: personal transformation, mindset shifts, relatable moments

1. **COMFORT ZONE**, Life is okay but something is missing
   > *"X days/months/years ago I was a normal [age] guy/girl, working as [role], just living life. I wasn't unhappy but it always felt like something was missing."*

2. **TRIGGER**, An event causes a realization
   > *"Then on a random [weekday], [trigger happened]"*

3. **CRISIS**, Realization: you're not your best self
   > *"But this mid-life crisis made me realize [valuable lesson]"*

4. **RECOVERY**, Action toward the better version
   > *"So I started to [actions taken]"*

5. **BETTER PLACE**, You're better off than the starting comfort zone
   > *"Which leads me to today. Now I [dream result], and [positive side effects from dream result]."*

---

### 3. ABOUT ME
Best for: brand intro video, new audience, pinned post

1. **INTRO**, Starting point
   > *"Hi I'm [name], and X months/years ago I was a normal guy/girl, working as [role], living a normal life."*

2. **CONFLICT**, What set you on this path
   > *"Well all up until [life-changing event]"*

3. **EPIPHANY**, What you realized
   > *"Because of this I realized [epiphany]"*

4. **CHANGE**, The action you took
   > *"Which led me to do [actions], and that leads me to today. Now I [dream result], and [positive side effects]."*

5. **PURPOSE**, Your deeper why
   > *"[Dream result] changed my life, and I made it my mission to help [target audience] go from [pain point] to [dream result] too."*

---

### 4. THE CHALLENGE
Best for: "I tried X for Y days/time" experiment content, proving a doubt wrong

1. One line asking: *"Is it possible to [do XYZ] in [insert time frame]?"*
2. One line: when you decided to try it, then *"here's what happened"*
3. One line explaining how you created an XYZ game plan
4. One line each explaining Step #1, Step #2, Step #3 of the plan
5. One line explaining the results from that game plan
6. One line explaining the positive, unexpected results that also came from it
7. One line explaining what the experience made you realize

### 5. THE BIG GOAL/DREAM
Best for: "why I started" / mission-driven content, long-term commitment stories

1. One line explaining how many times you've done [insert action] in your life (lifetime action count)
2. One line: ever since you were [X] years old, you've always wanted to do XYZ (childhood dream origin)
3. One line: on one random day you had [insert realization] and decided it was now or never (now-or-never moment)
4. One line explaining how you were feeling / what you were doing when you started
5. One line explaining the steps you decided to take
6. One line explaining the next step you have to take from where you're at now, in list style (what that involves)
7. One line explaining what's at stake and the constraints around it (e.g. a deadline) and the consequences if missed
8. CTA: ask people to follow to see what happens, or invite them to help with the next step

## When to Use
- **Hero's Journey** → client success story, testimonial-style content, case study Reel
- **Man in a Hole** → personal journey content, vulnerability post, mindset/breakthrough story
- **About Me** → brand intro video, first Reel for new account, pinned post script
- **The Challenge** → time-boxed experiment or "I tried X" content, myth-busting a doubt
- **The Big Goal/Dream** → mission/origin-story content that needs a longer arc than About Me, "why I'm doing this" posts
