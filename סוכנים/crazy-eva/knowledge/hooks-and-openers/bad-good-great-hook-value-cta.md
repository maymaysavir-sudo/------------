# Bad vs Good vs Great: Hook, Value, CTA

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZ-wG1oOORj/
**Category**: hooks-and-openers
**Added**: 2026-06-25

## Core Insight
The gap between a 100-view video and a 1M-view video is the quality of three levers run side by side: the hook, the value, and the CTA. Each can be bad, good, or great, and upgrading all three is what scales reach.

## How It Works
- Three clones labeled BAD (100 views), GOOD (100K), GREAT (1M) demonstrate the same three elements at each quality tier.
- Hook: bad -> good -> great (a great hook is specific, high-stakes, and curiosity-driving).
- Value: bad -> good -> great (great value is concrete and genuinely useful, not generic).
- CTA: bad -> good -> great. A bad CTA is just "Follow!"; a great CTA gives a reason to act and offers something (e.g. comment a keyword to receive a resource).

## Example
Great hook shown on screen: "If I Woke Up 30 Pounds Overweight Tomorrow and Had to Lose It All in 90 Days, This Is What I Would Do." Bad CTA shown: a bare "Follow!". The creator's own great CTA: "Comment SCRIPT for real scripts I wrote that got me over 1M views" (keyword-to-DM lead capture).

## When to Use
When auditing or rewriting a script: grade each of the three parts (hook, value, CTA) on the bad/good/great scale and push every weak one up a tier before posting. Especially useful for fixing a flat hook or a lazy "Follow!" CTA.
