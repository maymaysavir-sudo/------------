# 6 Hook Types Demonstrated Live

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYlUM_nzg1K/?igsh=MXczcWp5ZmsyeWRodw==
**Category**: hooks-and-openers
**Added**: 2026-05-24

## Core Insight
One reel can layer multiple hook types simultaneously, visual, verbal, title, pattern interrupt, text, and re-hook are shown happening at the same time, in real time, within the same video.

## How It Works
- **Visual hook**: camera slowly zooms in at the start
- **Verbal hook**: opens with a question ("Do you see how...")
- **Title hook**: text appears above the head at the beginning of the video
- **Pattern interrupt**: doing something unexpected while speaking (lifting a dumbbell mid-sentence)
- **Text hook**: text appears underneath the chin mid-video
- **Re-hook**: camera angle changes mid-video to re-grab attention that may have drifted

## Example
The creator demos all 6 hooks inside the same 20-second reel, the reel itself IS the example, making it immediately learnable by observation.

## When to Use
When teaching hook concepts to an audience, showing hooks in action beats explaining them in theory. Also useful as a checklist: before posting, ask which of these 6 elements your reel uses.
