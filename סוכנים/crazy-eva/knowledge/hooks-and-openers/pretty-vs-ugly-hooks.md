# Pretty vs. Ugly Hooks - Function Beats Aesthetics

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DULQxaQkVun/
**Category**: hooks-and-openers
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
Viral hooks are not aesthetic, pretty, or on-trend. Trying to make your hook look good is a trap, effective hooks are often "ugly" (not visually polished) but they work. Focus on proven viral hook structures, not how the hook looks.

## How It Works
**Pretty but ineffective:** Visually clean, well-styled text hook, but soft, generic, or vague
**Ugly but effective:** Visually messy or blunt, but specific, relatable, and creates immediate tension

**Examples (same topic, pretty → ugly):**
- "Investing for your future" → "Everyone tells you to invest in a Roth IRA in 2025 but no one actually shows you how to do it"
- "Home buying tips" → "The less money you put down for your home, the better interest rate you will receive from your bank"
- "Study tips" → "I study while listening to music. I listen to color noise to focus."
- "Weight loss advice" → "If I woke up 30 pounds overweight tomorrow and want to lose it all by summer, here's exactly what I'd do"

## When to Use
When a hook feels too polished or brand-y. Ask: does it create tension, specificity, or a "wait, really?" reaction? If not, make it uglier and more direct. Use viral-proven hook templates regardless of aesthetics.
