## Core Insight
Three proven hook template categories drive the most engagement: storytelling hooks, authority hooks, and comparison hooks. Eva packages these as a DM-gated lead magnet (CTA: "comment HOOK"), making the reel itself a live demonstration of the hook-to-lead pipeline.

## How It Works
- **Storytelling hooks**: Pull the reader into a narrative before revealing the value, curiosity-gap engine
- **Authority hooks**: Lead with a credibility signal or counterintuitive claim, instant positioning
- **Comparison hooks**: Create contrast (before/after, most people vs you, A vs B), forces a choice
- Each type maps to a different audience mindset and funnel stage
- The DM CTA ("comment HOOK, I'll send it over") demonstrates ManyChat keyword automation live

## Example

## When to Use
When teaching clients how hooks work, building a hook swipe file, or designing a lead-magnet DM automation. The 3-type taxonomy also maps funnel stages: storytelling/comparison = TOFU, authority = MOFU/BOFU.
