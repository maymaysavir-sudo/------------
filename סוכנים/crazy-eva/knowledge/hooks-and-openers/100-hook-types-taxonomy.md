# 100 Hook Types - Full Taxonomy

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DU5nfMjjazR/
**Category**: hooks-and-openers
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
There are at least 10 major hook categories, each with specific sub-types and templates. Knowing the full taxonomy means you never run out of hook angles for any piece of content.

## How It Works
Major hook categories:

1. **Comparison hooks**, taste, activity, price, product, time, metric comparisons + popular templates
2. **Tutorial hooks**, popular templates + one-off viral examples
3. **Transformation hooks**, personal before/after hooks + client/customer transformation hooks
4. **Educational hooks**, popular templates + creative one-offs
5. **Common mistakes / myth-busting hooks**, popular templates + one-off examples
6. **Tier list hooks**, ranking and rating formats
7. **Rating hooks**, numerical rating of things/ideas
8. **Ranking hooks**, ordered list format
9. **Cost breakdown hooks**, price/value reveal structure
10. **Challenge hooks**, dare/test format
11. **Storytelling hooks**, narrative openers that pull viewers in

## Example
Comparison: "I tried X vs. Y for 30 days, here's what happened"
Myth-busting: "Everyone says you need X to grow on Instagram. Here's why that's wrong."
Storytelling: "I almost quit social media last year. Here's what changed."

## When to Use
When writing hooks, pick a category based on your content type, then choose a sub-type. Use Eva's 1000-hook list (comment "HOOK" on her post) for pre-templatized versions of all of these.
