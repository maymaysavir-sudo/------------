# The If/Then Stacking Hook Formula

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DV8cxKQjaIn/
**Category**: hooks-and-openers
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
A repeatable "If [action], then [result]" hook structure that's currently going viral. Stack multiple rounds of it, each with new steps and a new dream result, to cover all angles of a problem and build toward an ultimate payoff.

## How It Works
- Start: "If you [action step 1] and [action step 2], then you [dream result 1]"
- Repeat: "If you [action step 3] and [action step 4], then you [dream result 2]"
- Keep stacking until all facets of the problem are covered → "the ultimate dream result"
- Works best with a visual demo of each step

## Example
- "If he places one hand on his back, grabs his elbow and rotates to the side, he will release his upper back"
- "If you can touch your ears with your hands and hold for one minute, hunchback posture starts to go away"
- "If you bite the pen and lift it 100 times every day, your jawline will become more defined"

## When to Use
Any niche with multiple actionable steps that each yield a tangible result, fitness, health, productivity, business, relationships. Especially powerful when you can demonstrate the action visually on camera.
