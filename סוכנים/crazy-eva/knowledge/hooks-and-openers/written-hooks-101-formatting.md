# Written Hooks 101 - Three Formatting Rules

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZkMXSbh-WH/
**Category**: hooks-and-openers
**Added**: 2026-06-16

## Core Insight
The on-screen text hook can make or break a regular video, and small formatting choices change readability and stopping power. Be extremely intentional about how the written hook looks, not just what it says.

## How It Works
- **Capitalization**: Use Title Case (capital letter at the start of each word) rather than ALL CAPS, easier to read and looks more intentional.
- **Two-line split**: Divide the hook into two lines instead of one long line, much easier to scan in the first second.
- **Delete filler words**: Cut every word that isn't essential; keep only the words that need to be there so the hook reads instantly.

## Example
Eva runs three A/B comparisons: Title Case vs ALL CAPS (Title Case wins), one line vs two lines (two lines wins because it's easier to read), and full sentence vs filler-words-removed (the trimmed version wins). She credits the creator whose video reminded her of these points.

## When to Use
Whenever you write the on-screen text hook for a Reel/TikTok, apply Title Case, a two-line split, and ruthless filler-word deletion before posting.
