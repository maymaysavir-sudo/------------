# Niche vs. Broad Hooks - Make Anyone Care

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYHf7tAgfN1/
**Category**: hooks-and-openers
**Added**: 2026-05-17

## Core Insight
A 100-view hook is *niche and makes no one care*. A 1M-view hook is *broad and makes everyone care*. Same topic, just reframed to widen who relates to it.

## How It Works
- Identify the niche version of your hook (specific product / specific symptom / specific audience)
- Rewrite to remove insider language and add a universal outcome ("make money", "feel better", "fix [universal problem]")
- Keep the specifics inside the video, only broaden the hook itself

## Example
- Niche: "My book review on Rich Dad Poor Dad" → Broad: "I've read 997 books on making money. Most are useless, but these 5 will make you rich."
- Niche: "Are you having less than 3 bowel movements per day?" → Broad: "I went from pooping once a week to once a day by making these 3 changes."
- Niche: "If you own a golden retriever who eats everything, teach them this behavior" → Broad: "Want a dog who won't eat anything that falls on the ground without your permission no matter what? Follow these steps."

## When to Use
Whenever a hook is underperforming. Test the niche-version first to vet the idea, then rewrite broad to scale reach without changing the core content.
