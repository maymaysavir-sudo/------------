# Comparison Hooks Drive Views

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DUqKIjdjVQY/ | https://www.instagram.com/p/DUN1f00ERqy/
**Category**: hooks-and-openers
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
Videos with comparison hooks consistently outperform the same video without them. The comparison format works in any niche because it forces the viewer to make a judgment before explaining the answer, creating instant engagement.

## How It Works
**6 types of comparison hooks:**
- Taste comparison (e.g., boiled vs. scrambled vs. fried eggs)
- Activity comparison (e.g., studying with music vs. color noise)
- Price comparison
- Product comparison
- Time comparison
- Metric comparison (e.g., posting 4x vs. 7x per week)

**Regular hook → Comparison hook (examples):**
- Regular: "Your anger is the key to making more money"
- Comparison: "Being proud of your anger is poor wisdom. Ignoring it is average. Noticing it and learning from it is excellent."
- Regular: "Eat at a calorie deficit"
- Comparison: "One Big Mac, large fry, large Coke... vs. daily double, no mayo, 6-count McNugget, Diet Coke"

**Proven viral templates:** Apply a comparison structure to any regular hook in your niche
**One-off viral hooks:** Riskier but worth using, outliers with no multiple verified backups but strong performance

## Example
A video asking "Which got more views, A or B?" across 3 different comparison pairs, all B videos had comparison hooks; all A videos did not.

## When to Use
When a video feels boring or flat. Add a comparison framing to the hook. Works in every niche.
