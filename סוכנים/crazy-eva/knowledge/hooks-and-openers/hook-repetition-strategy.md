# Same Hook, Viral Every Time - The Hook Repetition Strategy

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DWMpsfNBksm/?igsh=enVrODBnN3M4b2Z0
**Category**: hooks-and-openers
**Added**: 2026-03-25

## Core Insight
When top creators find a hook that works, they repeat it over and over until it stops working. The goal is to post until you find that hook, then double down and print results.

## How It Works
- Find a hook formula that goes viral → repeat it with different subjects/topics
- **Visual hook**, what viewers *see* in the first 3 seconds (recurring prop, setting, or visual pattern)
- **Verbal hook**, what viewers *hear* in the first 3 seconds (repeated opening phrase with swapped variable)
- **Written hook**, what viewers *read* in the first 3 seconds (on-screen text formula)
- **Stack multiple types**, combine visual + verbal for stronger retention

## Example
"What makes [nationality] women's faces so attractive?", same hook formula used 9 times across different nationalities, went viral every single time.

## When to Use
Once a video performs well, identify the hook formula, isolate the repeatable variable, and systematically reuse it across different topics or angles in the same niche.
