# Cuts Per Second (CPS) - The Visual Hook Rule

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DTxg4KDjfdx/?igsh=YTJuMDhoa3E1eHlu
**Category**: hooks-and-openers
**Added**: 2026-04-19

## Core Insight
High-view videos consistently have 3-6 shots or cuts within the hook. Single-shot hooks underperform. Visual variety in the first few seconds grabs attention before the viewer can scroll.

## How It Works
- **CPS = Cuts Per Second**, the number of distinct shots or cuts within your hook (first 2-3 seconds)
- High-view hook: 3-6 cuts in the hook
- Low-view hook: 1 shot (static or single angle) in the hook
- Adding multiple cuts is one of the simplest changes you can make to increase views

## Example
Eva showed 3 pairs of A/B videos, same topic, different CPS. In every pair, the higher-CPS version won more views. Pattern held across niches.

## When to Use
Every reel or short-form video. Apply during editing: even if you only have one camera, cut between different angles, zooms, or B-roll within the hook section.
