# Format Stacking

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DUS_nwFDfGZ/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
The best talking-back-and-forth videos don't just use one format, they stack multiple engaging formats on top of each other. Format stacking is what separates high-performing videos from average ones that use the same base format.

## How It Works
**Base format:** Talking back and forth (smart/dumb character)

**Add one or more of these on top:**
1. **Multitasking**, doing something unrelated in the video while delivering the script (natural/covert engagement)
2. **Visual**, props, objects, or movements that are *related* to the script content
3. **Editing notes**, visuals edited onto the screen showing what's being talked about

**Rule:** Video A (talking back and forth only) vs. Video B (talking back and forth + multitasking) → B wins almost every time.

## Example
Two videos, same talking-back-and-forth format. Video B adds multitasking (doing something unrelated while speaking). Video B outperforms Video A consistently.

## When to Use
Any time you film a talking-back-and-forth video. Always add at least one stacking layer. Also applies beyond this format, stack natural + unnatural formats whenever possible.
