# Plan a Full Month of Content in 45 Minutes

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXAJmtDv3Er/?igsh=MTk3a3B3bDcycHptdw==
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-16

## Core Insight
A full month of content can be planned in 45 minutes using a 5-step system: set your posting match, schedule, topics, content types, then fill the calendar. Structure removes the overwhelm.

## How It Works
1. **Posting match**, choose your level: Easy / Moderate / Hard (how many posts per week)
2. **Posting schedule**, pick days (e.g. Mon-Fri). Days don't matter much, it's just for accountability
3. **Content topics**, write your niche + 3-5 subtopics related to it
4. **Content types**, create a graph mapping your subtopics to content formats (educational, story, tips, etc.)
5. **Plan the month**, fill your calendar using your favorite topic + type combinations from the graph

## Example
Niche: personal branding. Subtopics: content creation, AI tools, mindset, audience growth, monetization. Graph maps each subtopic to formats. Month fills itself from combinations.

## When to Use
When feeling stuck on what to post, overwhelmed by content planning, or starting a new month with a blank calendar. Run this process once and have a full plan in under an hour.
