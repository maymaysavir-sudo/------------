# 60-Minute Content Ideas Speed Run

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaaRJp5Dop7/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-07-10

## Core Insight
A full month of content ideas can be generated in a 60-minute structured brainstorm sprint. Speed is the point, constraint forces output; you ideate first, quality-filter second, and never stare at a blank calendar again.

## How It Works
- Time-box ideation to 60 minutes, the constraint forces volume
- Start with your niche and 3-5 sub-topics as seed categories
- For each sub-topic, generate ideas across formats (educational, story, opinion, tips)
- Use the 5x rule: find what's already working in your niche and adapt the angle
- Do not filter or judge during the sprint, capture everything, edit after
- Output: a full content calendar's worth of ideas in one sitting

## Example
Eva's challenge hook: "If you had 60 minutes to create a month's worth of content ideas, how would you do it?", the prompt format itself is a high-engagement hook that drives comments.

## When to Use
When a creator has blank-calendar anxiety or is starting from scratch. Pair with the "Plan Full Month in 45 Minutes" scheduling system, ideation sprint first, then scheduling.
