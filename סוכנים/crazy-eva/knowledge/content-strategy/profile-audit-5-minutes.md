# Profile Audit in 5 Minutes - Quick Checklist

**Source**: PBL (Personal Brand Launch) by Ava Yuergens, "Audit Profile" PPTX
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-05-05

## Core Insight
A profile converts (or doesn't) in under 3 seconds. Run this 4-element audit on any account before posting content, if any element is missing or weak, fix it first.

## How It Works

**#1 USERNAME**
- Should be your name or business name
- Memorable, searchable, no unnecessary numbers/underscores

**#2 TAGLINE (Name field)**
- Format: `[Name] | [Occupation/Niche]`
- The name field is searchable, use keywords your audience types

**#3 BIO, 4-Line Framework**
1. What you do / sell and who you help
2. How people work with you / buy from you
3. Credibility line or result you've generated
4. CTA (direct to link)

**#4 LINK**
- Must go to a landing page for your main product, service, or lead magnet
- Not your homepage, a specific conversion page

## When to Use
Before launching any new account. Also use as a first-step client onboarding audit, fix the profile before spending a single hour on content.
