# Hijacking Viral Script Structures from Other Niches

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DUBAUHqDcmK/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
Trends are just script structures at their core. When a video goes viral in one niche, a smart creator can extract the underlying structure, templatize it, and apply it to their own niche, getting viral results without starting from zero.

## How It Works
1. See an obvious script structure go viral (in any niche)
2. Extract the blank template: "_____ is bad. _____ is good. _____ is excellent."
3. Adjust the blanks to fit your niche
4. Post it **early**, the earlier you recognize and recreate, the more reach you capture

**Easy vs. complex structures:**
- Easy: pattern is obvious when you watch (e.g., bad/good/excellent ranking format)
- Complex: hard to spot, but still highly effective, train yourself to look for them

**Critical rule:** Only recreate structures that have educational or value-driven content. Never repeat fluffy structures, they drive bad-quality followers and zero conversion.

## Example
Fitness niche: "Fried eggs are bad for weight loss. Scrambled eggs are good. Boiled eggs are excellent."
→ SMM niche: "Posting randomly is bad for growth. Posting 4× is good. Posting 7× is excellent."
Same structure, different niche, went viral in both.

## When to Use
Ongoing, build a habit of watching viral content outside your niche and asking "what's the underlying structure here?" Add it to your content backlog and recreate within the same week.
