# Reaction Video Format for Authority Building

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DURE23eDYdp/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
Reaction videos go viral across all niches because they piggyback on existing attention (a viral video) while positioning you as the expert who can evaluate it. The key difference between a good and bad reaction video is whether you add genuine professional value, not just commentary.

## How It Works
**The format:**
1. Find a viral outlier in your niche
2. Film a video reacting to it
3. Give your professional opinion or valuable advice, either during or after the reaction

**Bad reaction video:** Reacts to a viral video without adding much input or additional value (Eva admits she's done this)

**Good reaction video:** Reacts AND gives professional opinion / actionable advice

## When to Use
When you want to establish authority in your niche and a viral video has already captured attention. Great for doubling down on trending topics while staying educational. Use sparingly, pair with original content, don't make it your whole strategy.
