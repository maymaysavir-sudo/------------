# Content Maxing 101 - Core Principles

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXFebA5Achp/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-24

## Core Insight
Content creation in 7 principles, the most important one is the last: stop consuming content about content and just go create.

## The Principles

1. **Text hook immediately on screen**, don't make them read nothing for the first second
2. **Start in the middle of the story**, no buildup, drop the viewer into the action
3. **Keep videos under 30 seconds**, shorter is almost always better
4. **Make your voiceover different from the visuals**, they should add to each other, not repeat
5. **Be extreme. Whatever the topic, take a stance.**, milquetoast = forgettable
6. **Iteration is key, not just consistency**, if it's not working, do something different (but also post consistently)
7. **Stop consuming content about content. Just go create.**, the most important one

## When to Use
When you're stuck in research/planning mode and not creating, reset with this list. Also useful as a creative brief checklist before filming.
