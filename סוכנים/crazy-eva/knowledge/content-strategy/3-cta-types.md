# 3 CTA Types for Short-Form Video

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DU3Ch2gjjBE/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
Every video should end with one of three CTA types depending on your goal: grow your audience, capture a lead, or sell something.

## How It Works
- **Follow CTA**, ask the viewer to follow for more; use when you want audience growth
- **Freebie CTA**, offer something free in exchange for a comment/DM/email; use when you want leads in your pipeline
- **Paid offer CTA**, direct pitch to a product/service; use when the video already demonstrated value and credibility

## When to Use
Match the CTA to the funnel stage of the video: TOFU videos → Follow CTA, MOFU videos → Freebie CTA, BOFU videos → Paid offer CTA. Never end a video without one.
