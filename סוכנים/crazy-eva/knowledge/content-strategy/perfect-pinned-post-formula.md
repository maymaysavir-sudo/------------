# Perfect Pinned Post Formula - by Creator Type

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DWKE2OThq4g/?igsh=MTJzOTB4bnZleTByZA==
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-03-25

## Core Insight
Don't pin your most viral videos, pin the 3 posts that convert new profile visitors into followers or leads, tailored to your creator type.

## How It Works

**Service-based businesses / coaches:**
1. My story / founder story, how you got into the business
2. "How we work together" Reel, step-by-step process, real client example, or before/after
3. Best educational Reel, viral, hyper-actionable value

**Product-based businesses:**
1. My story / founder story
2. Product creation story, start-to-finish how the product was made
3. Before & after results, personal or customer using the product

**Educational content creators:**
1. My story, how you got into what you teach
2. About / mission post, what the page is about and why you post free content
3. Best educational Reel posted so far

## Example
A social media coach would pin: (1) their story of going from 0 to clients, (2) a "here's how I work with clients" process Reel, (3) their most-viewed educational tip Reel.

## When to Use
When setting up or refreshing your Instagram profile, the 3 pinned posts are the first impression for every new profile visitor.
