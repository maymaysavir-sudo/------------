# Cult Brand Framework - Purpose, Enemy, Leader

**Source**: https://vt.tiktok.com/ZS9vTEd9S/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-05-17

## Core Insight
If your product is great but nobody is buying, you don't have a cult message they connect with. Cult brands aren't built on logos or offers, they're built like religions, with three elements: a Purpose, an Enemy, and a Leader.

## How It Works
- **Purpose**, why you exist beyond making money; what bigger change you want in the world. Humans are hardwired to want to be part of something bigger than themselves
- **Enemy**, the thing that stops your purpose from becoming reality. Must be something your audience already fears or resents. Activates in-group / out-group psychology
- **Leader**, a credible character who fights the enemy and embodies the purpose. Doesn't have to be the founder, just someone who lives the mission
- When a brand aligns with a purpose people feel and an enemy people already hate, the audience feels seen → they join a movement, not a brand

## Example
**Nike**. Purpose: motivating people to achieve their goals (not selling shoes). Enemy: laziness and excuses. Leader: rotating cast of athletes who embody the fight.

## When to Use
When a brand has strong product/offer but flat sales, or content that "performs" but doesn't build attachment. Use as the foundation under every piece of branded content, every post should reinforce purpose, name the enemy, or showcase the leader.
