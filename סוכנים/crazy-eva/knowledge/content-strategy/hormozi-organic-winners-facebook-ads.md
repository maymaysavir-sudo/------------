# Turn Organic Winners Into Facebook Lead Ads (Hormozi Method)

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZcdwj4hvg2/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-06-12

## Core Insight
Instead of creating ads from scratch, run your best-performing organic posts as Facebook lead ads using "use existing post". The post keeps all its likes, comments, and shares when it runs as an ad, so the built-in social proof helps it convert. Eva credits the approach to Hormozi and says it was the first time Facebook ads ever worked for her after years of organic-only lead gen.

## How It Works
- Step 1, find posts: go through your account and pick the best performers, preferably the ones with the most saves or leads
- Step 2, set up the ad: Ads dashboard, plus create, Leads objective, continue. At the ad set level select your Meta Pixel under dataset and set daily budget to $5-10. At the ad level select your Instagram/Facebook profile, choose "use existing post", click select post, Instagram tab, paste the post shortcode ID, select it, enter. Add the website URL where leads should go, set the CTA (she uses Book a Call), turn off advanced creative, review everything, go live
- Step 3, duplicate: repeat the setup for 5-10 videos to start
- Step 4, test: over the next few days watch which ads get leads, increase budget on winners slowly (only once per day). After about a week a clear outlier emerges; keep putting money behind it. Kill ads that do not make money after a few weeks at low budget
- Ongoing: keep posting organic, keep feeding new posts into the system, because ads fatigue over time and you always want to be hunting for new outlier ads

## Example
Eva, who had failed at Facebook ads every previous attempt, used this on her own account (personalbrandlaunch) with Book a Call as the CTA and says these ads "converted so well" for her.

## When to Use
When you already have organic content that performs (saves, leads, comments) and want to scale lead gen with paid. Not a fit if you have no proven organic content yet; she explicitly says the method depends on starting with good organic posts.
