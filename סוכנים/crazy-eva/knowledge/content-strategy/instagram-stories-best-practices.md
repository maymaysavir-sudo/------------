# Instagram Stories Best Practices

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DUGHeJ6DnMT/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
Stories are a separate discipline from Reels. Most people use them wrong, either posting too many at once, using the wrong proof type, or never studying what works. The best Stories creators double down on what performs and always show their face.

## How It Works
**Posting cadence:**
- Don't: post multiple back-to-back → wait for them to expire → post one slide
- Do: post multiple stories together (consistent presence)
- Sales stories: 3-7× per week (not 3× per day)

**Proof/testimonials:**
- Don't: only written testimonials
- Do: before-and-after images that show results

**Text on stories:**
- Don't: big paragraphs / blocks of text
- Do: as little text as possible, just enough context

**Optimization:**
- Don't: ignore results
- Do: double down on best-performing templates, CTAs, and social proof formats

**Face:**
- Don't: never show your face
- Do: use your face whenever possible

## When to Use
When setting up a client's Stories strategy or auditing underperforming Stories. Start by identifying what CTA/proof combo got the best replies or link clicks, then double down.
