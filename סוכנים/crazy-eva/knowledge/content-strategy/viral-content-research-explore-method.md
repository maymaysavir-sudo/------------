# Viral Content Research - Instagram Explore Method (4 Hours)

**Source**: PBL (Personal Brand Launch) by Ava Yuergens, "Viral Content in 4 Hours" PDF
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-05-05

## Core Insight
Use Instagram Explore to quickly build a niche creator database, collect their top viral reels, and extract proven hooks and topics, all in one session. Then post 1 video/day using their structures with your own expert value.

## How It Works
1. Go to Instagram Explore page
2. Search your niche keyword
3. Find the top 10 creators in your niche
4. Screenshot their profiles + hit Follow
5. Instagram will suggest more accounts in your niche
6. Click through the suggested profiles
7. Follow and screenshot every creator with a big following that fits your niche (aim for 30-50 total)
8. Add all their usernames to a spreadsheet
9. Go to each profile and find their top 5-10 viral reels
10. Copy/paste the reel URLs under each username in the spreadsheet
11. (Optional) For each reel, write out: hook, topic, layout/format, view count, likes
12. Use their viral hooks and topics in your own videos, add your expert advice as the value

## Example
Niche: social media marketing → search "social media" on Explore → find creators with 50k-500k followers → screenshot 40 profiles → collect 200-400 top reels → spreadsheet has ~10 proven hooks per creator

## When to Use
Content planning sprint, run once before a filming batch to fill your script queue with proven topics and hooks. Also use when starting with a new niche or when content is underperforming.
