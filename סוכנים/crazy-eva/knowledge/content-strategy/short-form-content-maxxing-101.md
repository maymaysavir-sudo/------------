# Short Form Content Maxxing 101 - The Principles (incl. 70/20/10 Split)

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZNBSerNTQO/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-06-06

## Core Insight
A rapid-fire "101" of short-form content rules, anchored by the **70/20/10 content split**: 70% of your content should be formats already virally proven in your niche, 20% doubling down on *your own* videos that already hit, and 10% brand-new ideas. Across all of it: idea is king, you are one reputation, and recreate what works over and over.

## How It Works
- **Use proven viral hooks** in your videos, don't invent hooks from scratch; borrow ones already shown to work.
- **Educational + Personal = Personal Brand.** Expert education content plus personal storytelling/documentation content combine into a personal brand. People who like *you* like whatever you post, but this takes years to build.
- **Recreate your viral videos over and over.** Take your own winner (e.g. a 99.2M-view "OG" video) and remake it repeatedly ("double down").
- **Steal structure, not content.** Transcribe viral video scripts from online, turn them into a *fill-in-the-blank* template (separate Storytelling script structures vs Educational script structures), then drop your topic into the structure.
- **70 / 20 / 10 split** (credited to **Caleb Ralston, @calebralston**): 70% virally-proven-in-niche · 20% double down on your own viral videos · 10% new ideas.
- **You = one reputation, don't mess it up.**
- **Learn to post when you're busy.** You'll always be busy, so build the ability to keep posting through it.
- **Idea is king.** A video can be filmed in a boring way, but if the idea is good it will still perform, production quality matters far less than the idea.

## Example
Each rule is backed on-screen by grids of real videos with view counts: a 99.2M OG fitness video remade into a wall of 1M+ "double down" versions; Educational examples (1.3M / 2.6M / 1.2M) + Personal examples (1.1M / 2.5M / 1.1M) shown literally adding up to "Personal Brand"; the 70/20/10 pie chart drawn slice-by-slice next to matching video grids (70% niche-proven, 20% Italian-guy lookalike remakes at 7.7M/11M, 10% new ideas at 342K-1.4M).

## When to Use
As a checklist when planning a short-form content mix or auditing why an account isn't growing. Use 70/20/10 to allocate your posting calendar, lean on proven hooks + fill-in-the-blank script structures for the 70%, recycle your own winners for the 20%, and reserve only 10% for experiments, while keeping idea quality, not production polish, as the priority.
