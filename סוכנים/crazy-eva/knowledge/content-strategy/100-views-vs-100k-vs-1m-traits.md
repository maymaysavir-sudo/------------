# 100 Views vs. 100K vs. 1M - Video Trait Breakdown

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DWJUxOphGN5/?igsh=MTJjYmN3ZjNzMGxrcQ== (also: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaSjvHkMyd9/ - compact 6-element pre-publish checklist)
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-03-25

## Core Insight
Specific traits separate 100-view, 100K-view, and 1M-view videos. Most creators are stuck at the wrong tier because of a few fixable decisions.

## How It Works
- **Hashtags**, 100-view: uses for views; 1M-view: uses as keywords only
- **Script structure**, 1M-view: uses script structures from viral videos
- **Hook source**, 100K: proven viral hooks following the 5x rule; 1M: 1M+ view hooks (written, verbal, visual)
- **Subtitles**, 100-view: no subtitles; 1M-view: has subtitles
- **Content type**, 100K: educational only; 1M: educational + storytelling combined
- **Audio**, 100-view: trending audios (this is a trap, it signals low effort)
- **Format**, 100K: one engaging format; 1M: stacks multiple engaging formats
- **CTA**, 1M-view: ManyChat CTA with a freebie
- **Posting time**, 100-view: posts at a specific time (over-optimizing the wrong thing)

## Example
A 100-view creator posts educational content with trending audio at a "peak time" using hashtags for reach. A 1M-view creator combines education + story, uses a 1M-view hook, stacks formats, adds subtitles, and drives to a ManyChat freebie.

## Pre-Publish 6-Element Checklist (companion reel)
A tighter companion frame: before posting, check the six controllable elements every video lives or dies on - **written hook, verbal hook, visual hook, script, format, CTA**. If a video underperforms, walk these six to find the weak lever (these map onto the tier traits above: hook source, script structure, format, CTA).

## When to Use
When auditing an underperforming video, identify which tier its traits belong to and systematically upgrade toward 1M-view traits.
