# 5 Content Post Types Framework

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYeqr1pNXid/?igsh=MTkzMnNyYzRxdG5nNA==
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-05-21

## Core Insight
Every social media post should fall into one of 5 categories. Mapping your content calendar to these types ensures a mix of educational, personal, authoritative, and viral content.

## How It Works
**1. Educational**: Common mistakes/myths · Step-by-step tutorials · Comparisons · Do vs Don't / Right vs Wrong · Frameworks/acronyms/formulas · Quick tips & hacks

**2. Storytelling**: Big goals/dreams · Challenges · Lessons learned · Breakthroughs · About me / My journey

**3. Authority**: Personal transformations · Client transformations · "If I were to start [X] from scratch" · "What I would do for [big brand]" videos

**4. Series**: Daily series · Progress updates · Lesson series · Step-by-step series

**5. Double Down**: Replicate a viral video keeping everything the same except slightly adjusting the value · OR keep everything the same except the format

## Example
Viral tutorial video → double down by keeping topic/hook the same but filming as a vlog instead of talking-head, OR same format but add one more tactical step.

## When to Use
Content planning sessions; when stuck on what to post; to audit if content calendar is too heavy on one type; to plan a series strategy.
