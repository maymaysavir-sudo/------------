# What Type of Content Should You Post? (Choose by Tradeoff)

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaK0NKPRigo/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-07-05

## Core Insight
There is no single "best" content type. You pick your content type by deciding which tradeoff you're willing to make, then match the format to that choice. A rapid-fire "if you want X, post this / if you want Y, post that" decision framework.

## How It Works
Run through these binary tradeoffs and let your answers pick your content type:
- **Effort vs results**: low-effort, easy to script/film/edit (decent results) OR high-effort, harder to produce (typically better results).
- **Volume vs polish**: post consistently 7-14x/week (less polished) OR polished content only 3-5x/week.
- **Speed vs depth**: faster results OR slower but more meaningful results.
- **Easy planning vs creative independence**: post what's currently working/trending (planning is easy) OR create independently, not based on what's working (more creative).
- **Niche learning style**: visual niche where people need to SEE to learn OR verbal niche where people need to HEAR to learn.

## Example
A busy solo founder who wants leads fast with minimal production picks: low-effort + high-volume (7-14x/week) + fast results + post-what's-working. A premium expert brand picks: high-effort + polished 3-5x/week + slower-but-meaningful + creatively independent. (In the reel each choice maps to a specific on-screen content-type example.)

## When to Use
When a client or you can't decide what to post, or feel scattered across formats. Use it to convert vague "what should I post?" paralysis into a few clear tradeoff decisions, then commit to the matching content type.
