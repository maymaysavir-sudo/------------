# SMM Agency Process - 6 Steps (PBL Workflow)

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DWzRs7ZD1wi/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
Eva's agency (Personal Brand Launch) follows a 6-step process that separates research and scripting from filming, so the client only needs to show up and film, while the agency handles everything else.

## How It Works
1. **Onboarding**, client fills out an onboarding doc (all about them + their business); then a 3-way call with client, customer success manager, and account manager to finalize
2. **Research**, 8-10 hours finding top creators in client's niche; pull their 5-10 most viral videos; study hooks, topics, formats in a spreadsheet
3. **Script**, write 15-30 scripts using viral hooks/topics from research + onboarding doc + client's own content assets (so scripts sound authentic to the client)
4. **Film**, client films with their phone
5. **Edit**, agency takes raw footage, adds captions, cuts, formatting
6. **Upload**, post across all short-form platforms and monitor growth/leads

## Example
Client provides their onboarding doc → agency does all research and scripting → client films 15-30 videos in one batch → agency edits and posts over weeks.

## When to Use
As a reference for building or pitching an SMM service offering. Also useful as a content strategy framework: research → script → film → edit → post is the right order, not the reverse.
