# Content Mix Ratios: Leads vs. Followers Goal

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DUlMR_AjlpU/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
Two accounts posting the same volume should have completely different content mixes depending on whether their goal is more leads or more followers. Getting this ratio wrong means spending effort on content that doesn't serve your actual goal.

## How It Works
Both examples assume **30 videos/month**:

**Goal: MORE LEADS**
- 10 videos → TOFU (easy educational, simple structures, double-downs on high-view + high-lead videos), CTAs: follow + ManyChat comment → freebie
- 5 videos → MOFU (storytelling), CTAs: follow
- 15 videos → BOFU (hyper educational), CTAs: ManyChat comment → freebie + paid offer

**Goal: MORE FOLLOWERS**
- 15 videos → TOFU (easy educational, simple structures, double-downs on highest-follower videos), CTAs: follow + occasional ManyChat → freebie
- 7 videos → MOFU (storytelling), CTAs: follow
- 8 videos → BOFU (hyper educational), CTAs: follow

## When to Use
When setting up a monthly content calendar. Start by defining the goal (leads or followers), then apply the matching ratio. Adjust BOFU CTA from follow → paid offer as audience warms up.
