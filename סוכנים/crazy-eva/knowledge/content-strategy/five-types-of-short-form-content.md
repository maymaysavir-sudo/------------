# The 5 Main Types of Short-Form Content (and Their Sub-Types)

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZ29-0jBD9i/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-06-23

## Core Insight
All short-form content falls into five main types: educational, storytelling, authority, series, and double down. The best creators on the platform do not pick one, they post all five.

## How It Works
- Type 1, Educational: value-driven content about your niche. Six sub-types: 1) tutorial (step-by-step process), 2) comparison (compare different actions or methods to get a result), 3) myth-bust or common mistake (state the myth or mistake and correct it), 4) dos vs don'ts or right vs wrong, 5) niche quick tip or hack, plus any framework, acronym, or formula. Creators: Alex Hormozi, Codie Sanchez, Jeff Nippard.
- Type 2, Storytelling: personal stories about you or your business. She uses templated storytelling structures (Big Goal/Dream, Challenge, Breakthrough, About Me, Hero's Journey, Man in a Hole). Creators: Ryan Trahan, Brydie Drake.
- Type 3, Authority: social proof and results-focused content that establishes you as the expert. Ways to make it: 1) fake case study (step-by-step on how you would do something for a new or existing business), 2) personal transformation (your own before to after), 3) client transformation (someone else's before to after).
- Type 4, Series: a repeatable thread of content, technically optional but working very well on social media for the past year or so.
- Type 5, Double Down: templatize one of your viral reels into a repeatable format you post over and over. Creators: Christopher with his sauces, Sasha with her bagel videos, Ashen with his routine videos, Benjamin with his cooking-test videos.

## Example
On screen she shows a five-slice content wheel (educational, storytelling, authority, series, double down) and example posts under each sub-type, like a comparison video at 7.6M views (@maxthemeatguy ranking $1,000 vs $300 vs $50), a niche quick tip at 26.8M (@healthyemmie), a personal transformation at 5.9M (@charlizeechiu), and Benjamin's "double down" cookie-temperature series scaling from a 7.9M original to multiple 5M to 12.6M repeats.

## When to Use
When auditing your content mix or planning a calendar. Make sure you are posting across all five types, not just one, since the strongest accounts cover the full range.
