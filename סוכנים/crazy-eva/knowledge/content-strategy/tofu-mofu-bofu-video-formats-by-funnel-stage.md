# Which Video Format Belongs at Each Funnel Stage (TOFU / MOFU / BOFU)

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZqLUNWzfT0/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-06-23

## Core Insight
Every short-form video format has a natural funnel stage. Eva sorts common content formats into Top, Middle, and Bottom of funnel so you post the right format for the goal (reach vs trust vs conversion), and she backs each placement with real view-count receipts.

## How It Works
- TOFU (reach, cold audience): storytelling videos, tier-list videos, ranking/rating videos, "not super in-depth" educational, and "what I'd do if I was in X situation to get leads" hypotheticals. Broad and shareable (examples shown at 1M, 3.7M, 1.6M views).
- MOFU (trust, warming up): "day in the life" videos and in-depth educational content. These build credibility with people already aware of you.
- BOFU (conversion, ready to buy): personal transformation videos, client before/after testimonials, and hypothetical case studies of what you would do for a celeb or big brand. These prove results and push the decision.
- Some formats straddle stages: step-by-step one-off tips and hacks sit bottom-to-middle.

## Example
Storytelling reels shown pulling 1M / 432K / 473K views as TOFU reach plays; a "Rich Dad Poor Dad" tier-list video at 3.7M; a personal transformation video at 105K as a BOFU conversion play.

## When to Use
When planning a content calendar, pick the format that matches the job you need that post to do: reach (TOFU), trust (MOFU), or conversion (BOFU), instead of posting one type and expecting it to do everything.
