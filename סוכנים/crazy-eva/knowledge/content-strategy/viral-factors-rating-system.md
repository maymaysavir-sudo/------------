# Viral Factors Rating System - What Actually Moves the Needle

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DV87SeqDWxp/ · https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXSLGBHBOhf/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
Eva rates the most common "viral tactics" on a 0-10 scale, cutting through myths to show where effort should actually go.

## How It Works
- **Hashtags** → 0, useless if content isn't strong; maybe minor keyword signal at best
- **Written/verbal/visual hooks** → 10, the single most critical factor; if the hook doesn't land, nothing else saves the video
- **Engaging format** → 6, can elevate a weak script, but strong script can carry without it
- **Posting at a specific time** → 0, doesn't move results
- **Trending audio** → implied low priority (not rated as a main lever)
- **Doubling down on top performers** → 10, easiest path to another viral video if you already have one
- **Editing / captions** → 6, captions are mandatory (90% watch on mute); ADHD-style edits no longer effective

## Example
If you have a video that went viral, making a follow-up on the same topic/format is rated a 10, the highest ROI move available.

## When to Use
When prioritizing where to invest time in content production, especially when deciding between fancy editing, timing optimization, or nailing the hook.
