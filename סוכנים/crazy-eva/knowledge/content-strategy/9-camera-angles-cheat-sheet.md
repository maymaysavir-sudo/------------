# 9 Camera Angles Cheat Sheet

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DV3uuU7BXxd/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
Each camera angle signals something different to the viewer and serves a distinct storytelling purpose. Mix them throughout your video to stay engaging and hit the two-second rule.

## How It Works
- **Low angle**, builds authority (camera looks up at you)
- **Above angle**, storytelling or appearing smaller/vulnerable
- **Top-down**, food, tutorials, making/building something, products
- **Close shot**, telling a secret or something personal
- **Behind-the-shoulder**, showing screen/phone, reading, mirror, a view, watching something
- **Wide shot**, establishing a setting, or leaving room for editing notes
- **Medium shot**, talking directly/level with the viewer
- **Pan shot**, showing a setting or environment
- **Extreme close shot**, highlighting one specific part of something

## Example
Tutorial video: open with a wide shot (setting), cut to medium (intro), top-down for the demo, extreme close for the detail, close shot for the "secret tip", behind-the-shoulder for the screen walkthrough.

## When to Use
When planning shot variety for any video, especially useful alongside the two-second rule (a new shot/cut/effect every 2 seconds). Different angles = built-in engagement without extra editing.
