# Top 1% Video Structure - Percentile Framework

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DV54D7mBBsw/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
Each layer of your video structure unlocks a higher performance tier. Most creators stop at 50%, the top 1% nail all five layers plus the two-second visual rule.

## How It Works
- **Top 50%**, Line 1 raises curiosity and promises a payoff
- **Top 30%**, Line 2 bridges hook → meat, opens a curiosity loop, and introduces a solution
- **Top 10%**, The meat delivers actionable/measurable value the viewer can implement immediately
- **Top 5%**, CTA tells the viewer exactly what to do next
- **Top 1%**, Filmed in an engaging format with a cut, shot change, or effect every 2 seconds

## Example
The Two-Second Rule: every 2 seconds have something new, a cut, a shot change (long/medium/close/above/below), a zoom, or face tracking. More cuts = more engagement.

## When to Use
When planning or reviewing any short-form video, use as a checklist to diagnose which tier your content is hitting and what's missing to level up.
