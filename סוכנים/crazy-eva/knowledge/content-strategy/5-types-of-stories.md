# 5 Types of Instagram Stories

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DV3TUQXhpuf/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
There are 5 distinct story types, each serving a different goal. Eva recommends posting predominantly sales stories, with personal ones sprinkled in occasionally.

## How It Works
- **Personal**, life, feelings, daily activities; builds connection and likeability
- **Sales, paid offer**, case studies, results, testimonials, before/afters → link to paid offer
- **Sales, lead magnet**, promote something free to collect email/contact info
- **Authority**, results, experiences, success, knowledge; builds credibility
- **Educational**, teach something useful; helps the audience learn or do better

## Example
Sales story: share a client before/after or testimonial screenshot, then add a link sticker to your paid offer.

## When to Use
When planning your Stories content mix. Default to sales stories (paid offer or lead magnet) as the backbone, add authority stories to build trust, and use personal stories sparingly to stay relatable.
