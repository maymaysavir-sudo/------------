# 4 Levels of Awareness

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DX4zL-kiRGz/?igsh=bzdlcmh4eGs1cjZh (also: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbjHcx0BKO9/ - adds real view-count examples per level)
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-05-05 (updated 2026-08-03)

## Core Insight
Most creators post product or solution-aware content without realizing it has the lowest reach potential. The highest-performing content targets people at awareness level 3 or 4, before they even know your product exists.

## How It Works
- **Level 1, Product Aware**: Videos about your product/service. Low reach, most people don't know what it is.
- **Level 2, Solution Aware**: Videos about niche solutions to known problems. Low reach, most people don't know these solutions.
- **Level 3, Problem Aware**: Videos about niche problems people know they have. High reach, most people can relate to their own problems.
- **Level 4, Unaware**: Videos about problems people didn't know were problems but can relate to. Highest reach, broadest relatable pool.

## Example
A social media coach posting "why my DM strategy doubled bookings" (Level 1) vs. "why you're posting every day and still not growing" (Level 4), the second hits a problem anyone with a stagnant account feels.

Real examples with view counts, showing the reach gap in practice:
- **Level 1, Product-Aware**: "I charge at least $5K a month for 10 social videos and here's exactly what my client gets" (202K) / a queso-seasoned pretzel bites demo (7,813)
- **Level 2, Solution-Aware**: "How can you bring the maxilla forward?" (153K) / "If your knees cave in every time you lunge or squat" (1.7M)
- **Level 3, Problem-Aware**: "If you can't dribble behind your back like that every single time you lose the ball" (1.5M) / "If your dog barks like this every time they see another dog" (530K) / "If your stomach looks like this or like this you're not fat, you probably just have an overworked liver" (1.5M)
- **Level 4, Unaware**: "If you had to guess, would you say relationships are better when the man is better looking than the woman?" (3.1M) / "The only difference between a supermodel and a normal person is tongue posture" (6.6M) / "There's a difference [between] someone that sits like this and then sits like this" (8.2M)

The view counts climb by roughly an order of magnitude from Level 1 to Level 4, showing the awareness-level effect isn't just theoretical.

## When to Use
When brainstorming video topics: start at Level 3 or 4 to maximize reach, then use Level 1-2 content only for warm audiences and BOFU conversions.
