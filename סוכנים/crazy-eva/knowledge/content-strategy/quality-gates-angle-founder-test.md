# Quality Gates Before Publishing - The Angle Test and the Founder Test

**Source**: https://github.com/muratcankoylan/ralph-wiggum-marketer (copywriter skill, harvested 2026-06-12, not installed)
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-06-12

## Core Insight
Two fast pass/fail gates to run on any piece of content before it goes out. They come from an autonomous AI copywriter system, but they work as manual checklist items for any post, Reel script, ad, or carousel.

## Gate 1 - The Angle Test (uniqueness)
Ask: could a generic competitor have published this exact piece? If yes, the angle is too generic, sharpen it before publishing.
- Generic: "AI will change marketing"
- Passing: "Why 73% of AI marketing tools fail, and the 3 patterns that predict success"
- The fix is usually specificity: a number, a named pattern, a contrarian claim, or a concrete before/after

## Gate 2 - The Founder Test (voice)
Ask: would you actually publish this under your own name without edits? If the honest answer is "almost", iterate again. The point is that matching the real voice matters more than producing a finished draft fast. Check the gate against your own voice profile, not against generic "good copy". If you do not have one yet, build it as you go: collect your own published posts and note the patterns.

## When to Use
As the last step before approving any content, after reel-algorithm scoring. reel-algorithm predicts scroll-stop performance; these two gates catch the two failure modes it does not: a piece that performs but sounds like everyone else (Angle), or performs but does not sound like you (Founder).
