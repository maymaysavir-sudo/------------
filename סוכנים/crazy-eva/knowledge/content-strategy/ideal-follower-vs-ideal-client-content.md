# Ideal Follower vs. Ideal Client Content

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DUauRlKDrUb/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
Stop creating content for your ideal buyer if you want to grow. Your ideal client already knows they have a problem and are ready to buy, but they're a tiny audience. Your ideal follower doesn't even know they have the problem yet. Broad content grows reach; niche content gets leads.

## How It Works
**Ideal client content** → used to get leads (BOFU)
- Knows the problem + solution
- Ready to buy
- Narrow audience

**Ideal follower content** → used for growth (TOFU)
- Doesn't know they have a problem
- Not ready to buy
- Massive audience

**Examples (narrow → broad):**
- Dentist: "myths about braces" → "dental hygiene routines"
- Interior designer: "best barstools" → "what to buy at IKEA"
- Workplace lawyer: "steps if terminated" → "3 signs your boss is toxic and what the law says"

## When to Use
When a client's account isn't growing despite quality content, they're likely only posting for buyers. Add broad TOFU content targeting curious non-buyers to drive follower growth.
