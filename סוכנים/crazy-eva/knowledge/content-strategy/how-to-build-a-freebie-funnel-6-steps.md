# How to Build a Freebie / Lead Magnet Funnel - 6 Steps

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/Da00ngwuuyL/ (also: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbGC_57szLC/)
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-07-17

## Core Insight
Freebie CTAs convert into paid clients at over 2x the rate of direct-to-offer CTAs, but this only works with an actually good freebie (not something thrown together with ChatGPT in two seconds) plugged into a proper funnel, not just a giveaway. A genuinely great freebie also builds trust: if the free thing is a banger, people assume the paid offer must be incredible, so they're far more likely to buy.

## How It Works
1. **Identify what your freebie could solve.** Brainstorm a list of problems it could solve, pick the most valuable one, and make sure it relates directly to your paid offer.
2. **Determine your freebie type.** Choose the delivery format that fits best (you can combine multiple types).
3. **Create your freebie.** Build it so it's shippable and ready to deliver, not a rushed placeholder.
4. **Create your funnel.** Publish two landing pages: an opt-in page (name + email required to get the freebie) and a thank-you/delivery page (with the freebie download and your paid offer pitched at the bottom).
5. **Create your keyword/DM automation.** Set up an automation (e.g. ManyChat-style) tied to a specific keyword, following the platform's keyword rules.
6. **Add it everywhere.** Put the landing page link in your bio, and reference the same CTA in Reels, carousels, and Stories.

## Example
The creator's own freebie has generated 100,000+ leads for her business; she offers it as a "comment HOOK and I'll send it over" example inside the same video, modeling the exact opt-in mechanic being taught.

**A/B demonstration (why fewer posts + a freebie wins):** Two business owners chase leads for one month.
- **Owner A** posts *more* and sells harder: 10-14 Reels/week, 7 Stories/week, 5 carousels/week, with 75% of posts CTA-ing straight to the paid offer / sales page.
- **Owner B** posts *less* and routes to a lead magnet: 5-7 Reels/week, 5 Stories/week, 2 carousels/week, with 50% of CTAs pointing to a freebie.
- Result: both got thousands of leads, but Owner B had more of those leads convert into paid clients, even though (because) they used a freebie. Direct-to-offer CTAs still work and are worth using, but freebie CTAs out-convert them into sales.

## When to Use
When your direct-to-offer CTA is underperforming, or you want a lower-friction top-of-funnel entry point that still routes toward a paid offer. Only worth building if you can make a genuinely valuable freebie, not a low-effort AI-generated placeholder.
