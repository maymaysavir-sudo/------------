# Batching vs. Daily Content Workflow

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DUGpPwPDtrf/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
Daily posting about current news requires daily research and burns you out. Batching educational/storytelling content once a month is sustainable, produces evergreen videos that never stop performing, and keeps you from burning out.

## How It Works
**Daily workflow (news/trend-based):**
- Research every day to stay current
- Script, film, edit, and post all in one day
- Videos stop performing when the trend dies
- High risk of burnout

**Batched workflow (educational/storytelling):**
- Research outlier hooks and topics once per month (an outlier hook from 6 months ago often still works today)
- Batch script, film, and edit all videos once per month
- Videos are evergreen, watch today or years from now
- Never burns out

## When to Use
Default to the batching workflow for any educational or storytelling content. Reserve daily posting only for genuinely time-sensitive niches (news, breaking trends). For SMM clients: this is the pitch for why educational content → sustainable long-term growth.
