# 1 Year of Content - The Multiplication Formula

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DVtfPHvCYRW/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
You never run out of content ideas if you multiply niche × sub-niches × formats × angles. 1 niche + 5 sub-niches + 10 formats + 7 angles = 350+ combinations, nearly a year of content before adding storytelling.

## How It Works
- 1 main niche
- × 5 sub-niches
- × 10 video formats (different engaging ways to film the same topic)
- × 7 video angles (camera angles, see 9 Camera Angles Cheat Sheet)
- = **350+ video combinations** (~1 year of content at 1 video/day)
- + storytelling content (wins/losses/experiences) = another ~month of content

## Example
Social media marketing niche → sub-niches: hooks, content strategy, editing, analytics, mindset → filmed as ranking video, tutorial, Q&A, case study, tip hack, tier list, comparison, storytelling, before/after, trend → shot from 7 different angles = hundreds of distinct videos.

## When to Use
When a client or creator says "I don't know what to post." Map out their niche → sub-niches → formats → angles and show them the math. The problem is never a lack of ideas, it's too much consuming and not enough creating.
