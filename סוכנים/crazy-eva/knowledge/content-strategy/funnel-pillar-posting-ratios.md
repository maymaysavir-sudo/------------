# Funnel Pillar Posting Ratios (Attract / Nurture / Convert)

**Source**: https://vt.tiktok.com/ZS9nLsPTQ/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-05-17

## Core Insight
The three pillars of content (attract / nurture / convert = top / middle / bottom of funnel) don't get equal weight by default. The right posting ratio depends on your current goal, growing, nurturing, or selling, and it shifts week to week.

## How It Works
- **All-three balanced goal** (growing + nurturing + converting all the time): post 6×/week → 2 attract / 2 nurture / 2 convert. Don't bunch them, every week needs all three so you hit different community members along their journey
- **Already grown, want sales** (focus on conversation + conversion): 6×/week → 2 attract / 3 nurture / 1 convert. Lean into middle + bottom of funnel
- **Growth phase** (just trying to grow): 60-70% top-of-funnel attract content, rest nurture/convert
- The first decision is always: *where am I now and what's my goal*, the ratio falls out of that

## Example
6 posts per week broken three ways: 2/2/2 for balanced, 2/3/1 for sell-mode, ~4/1/1 for pure growth mode.

## When to Use
When building a weekly content calendar or critiquing why posts aren't doing what you want. Mismatch between pillar mix and current goal is the most common reason content "works" but doesn't move the business.
