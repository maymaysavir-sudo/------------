# "Me Content" vs. "You Content" Framework

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DUf3C12EaBF/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
Every video you post is either about you (builds connection) or for your audience (builds authority). You need both to grow. Mixing them up, using the wrong format or CTA for the wrong type, kills the video's effectiveness.

## How It Works
**"Me Content"**, personable, builds audience connection with you:
- Storytelling videos
- Day in the life / series
- My story / founder stories
- Wins and losses
- Lessons, goals, challenges
- Formats: voiceover, shot/angle change, multitasking
- CTAs: follow + engagement (comments, likes)

**"You Content"**, gives value, builds authority in your niche:
- Educational videos (tutorials, tips, frameworks, acronyms, rules)
- Myths / common mistakes
- Comparisons
- Formats: clone, talking back and forth, visual, whiteboard, green screen, reaction
- CTAs: follow + lead (ManyChat comment → freebie)

## When to Use
Use this framework when planning your content calendar. Mix both types every week. If you want more leads, lean into "You content." If you want more connection/loyalty, lean into "Me content."
