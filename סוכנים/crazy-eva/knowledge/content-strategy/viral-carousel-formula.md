# What Makes a Viral Carousel (vs. a Dead One)

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DWRDxY1mr0J/?igsh=eDJ6aHc5ejNkYjVq
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-03-25

## Core Insight
The difference between a 100-view and 100K-view carousel comes down to 5 specific decisions: audio, hook source, format, slide count, and slide density.

## How It Works
- **Attach audio**, gets the carousel added to the Reels tab for significantly more reach
- **Hook from viral Reels**, use proven verbal and visual hooks from viral content on the cover slide, not a freestyle hook
- **Follow a proven format**, use one of the 8 best-performing carousel formats, not freeform
- **Slide count: 2-3 OR 15+**, avoid the dead zone of 5-10 slides
- **One idea per slide**, clean layout with blank space, not packed with information
- **Use visuals**, not text-only

## Example
100-view carousel: 7 slides, freestyle hook, text-only, packed with info.
100K-view carousel: 15+ slides, hook borrowed from a viral Reel, audio attached, one idea per slide with visuals.

## When to Use
Before creating any carousel, use as a checklist for structure, hook, and format decisions.
