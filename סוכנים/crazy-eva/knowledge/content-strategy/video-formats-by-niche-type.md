# Video Formats by Niche Type

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DWv8vVjBRVy/ (also: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbgimXuh2Rj/)
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
The best video format depends on how your audience learns. Visual niches need to see, verbal niches need to hear, product niches need both, and each maps to specific structures and formats.

## How It Works

**Visual niche** (audience needs to see to learn, fitness, cooking, art, etc.):
- Best structures: tutorials, transformations, educational tips/hacks
- Best formats: voiceover, shot/angle changes, visual demos

**Verbal niche** (audience needs to hear to learn, coaching, marketing, finance, etc.):
- Best structures: tutorials, educational tips/hacks, right vs. wrong
- Best formats: clones, talking back and forth, green screens, whiteboards, reactions

**Product niche** (audience needs to both hear and see, beauty, tech, fashion, etc.):
- Best structures: comparisons, before/afters, storytelling
- Best formats: voiceover, visual, clone, reaction

## When to Use
When deciding how to film a video, match your format to your niche type first, then layer in the content structure. Using the wrong format for your niche type reduces comprehension and retention.
