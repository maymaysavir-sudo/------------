# TOFU, MOFU, BOFU Content Framework

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DVvlIBlDT6t/ + https://www.instagram.com/p/DU03Daujn1V/ + https://www.instagram.com/p/DUqpVS7Dm-5/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
TOFU/MOFU/BOFU maps directly to content goals, views, followers, and leads respectively. Posting only one type = following with inconsistent views. Posting all three = personal brand with consistent views because people know, like, and trust you.

## How It Works
- **TOFU (Top of Funnel) → Views**: Broad, easy-to-understand content, ranking videos, rating videos, tier lists, Q&A style, founder/my story videos, educational comparisons, "what I would do for [celeb/big brand]" fake case studies, "what I would do in XYZ situation" videos
- **MOFU (Middle of Funnel) → Followers**: Storytelling about you and your business (wins/losses/experiences), personal before/afters, Q&A videos, educational one-off tips & hacks
- **BOFU (Bottom of Funnel) → Leads**: In-depth step-by-step tutorials, client/customer before/afters, authority-based content, fake case study videos (what you'd do for a celebrity or specific business)

## The Key Distinction
- Post **one type only** → following with fluctuating views
- Post **all three types** → personal brand with consistent views (audience knows, likes, and trusts you)

## Example
A social media marketer: "Top 5 Instagram mistakes" (TOFU for views) → "How I rebuilt my business after losing 3 clients in one month" (MOFU for followers) → "Step-by-step Reels strategy I used to get my client 10K followers" (BOFU for leads).

## When to Use
When planning your content calendar, identify what you need most right now (views, followers, or leads) and weight your mix accordingly. Default: all three. If views are low → add TOFU. If followers aren't converting → add more BOFU.
