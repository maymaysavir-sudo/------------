# The 5x Rule - Competitor Research Framework

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DWR0KjUDxym/?igsh=MThlcmg0eWZ6cHVx
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-03-25

## Core Insight
A video is "viral" if it gets 5x the account's follower count in views. Use this threshold to identify top-performing content in your niche, extract hooks and topics, then recreate with your own expertise as the value.

## How It Works
- Find 10-20 creators in your niche
- Identify videos that got 5x+ their follower count in views
- Log each in a spreadsheet: hook, view count, topic, filming format
- Repeat for each creator, aim for a few hundred top-performing reels total
- Use those hooks and topics in your own content, but make the value your own expertise and actionable advice

## Example
Creator has 10k followers → any video with 50k+ views qualifies as viral → add to research spreadsheet.

## When to Use
Content planning phase, run this research before scripting to ensure you're building on proven formats and hooks in your niche.
