# Ranking Viral Video Types by Lead Quality

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZ968w3yI9J/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-06-25

## Core Insight
Not all viral views are equal. Ranked by how well they convert into quality followers and leads (not just raw views), the best-to-worst order of viral video types is: Yap > Storytelling > Educational > Opinion > Trends/Memes.

## How It Works
- 1 - Yap content (BEST): low-effort, low-production talking-to-camera. Because production is minimal, the talking has to be genuinely valuable or personable (education or storytelling). If a low-effort video goes viral, those are the highest-quality views you can get. Recommendation: founders/owners should whip out the phone and film a thought immediately, no heavy pre-planning.
- 2 - Storytelling: extremely personable, so people fall in love with you, your brand, and your personality. People follow because they like YOU.
- 3 - Educational: what you say is so valuable people follow for the information, and it also generates leads.
- 5 - Opinion: often slides into rage bait (reacting meanly, or saying something just to provoke) or "crazy" hooks. High views but low-quality: fewer leads/followers per view.
- 6 - Trends/Memes (WORST): nothing personal about you, so they rarely convert to followers or leads.

## Example
Opinion-content example given by the creator: posting "I got engaged at 16, I got married at 19" hooks print huge views, but those views are low quality (few leads/followers) versus a valuable yap or educational video.

## When to Use
When deciding what content TYPE to prioritize: if the goal is quality leads and followers (not vanity views), lean into yap, storytelling, and educational over opinion bait and trend-chasing.
