# 3 Things To Do After Every Post

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DS5hjnigOus/?igsh=ZW5ubjExamQzNDE4 · https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXcdyMxDEUJ/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-19

## Core Insight
After posting, use the IG Edits app to track analytics, measure view-to-follower ratio, and study your retention graph, so every flop becomes data for the next video.

## How It Works
- **Step 1, Download IG Edits app**:
  - Connect your Instagram account
  - Click the 3-bar button → click on your videos for advanced analytics tracking
- **Step 2, Analyze the data**:
  - Key metric: "Followers from video"
  - Use the view-to-follower ratio equation to score how well the video did at converting views → followers
  - Based on this, decide whether to make more videos in this style
- **Step 3, Study the retention graph**:
  - In the Edits app, look at the retention graph shape
  - Match it to known retention graph patterns → diagnose what went wrong
  - If the topic was good but the video flopped: study the graph, find the drop-off point, fix it
  - Same concept + different virally-proven hook = better performance

## Example
Eva posted a reel that she knew had a solid topic but it flopped. She used the retention graph to diagnose the problem, then re-uploaded the same concept with a different hook, it performed significantly better.

## When to Use
After every post. Makes iteration systematic instead of guesswork, especially useful when the topic is right but results are wrong.
