# TOFU / MOFU / BOFU - Three Content Types and Their Goals

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXui_NmjJyY/ (also: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DanJqYNBzMx/)
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-30

## Core Insight
There are only three types of content, defined by funnel stage. Each one has its own goal and its own recommended formats, picking the wrong format for your goal is why content underperforms.

## How It Works
**TOFU (top of funnel)**, broad, great for **views**.
- Ranking videos
- Rating videos
- Tier list videos
- Q&A style videos
- "My story" / founder videos
- Easy-to-understand educational videos

**MOFU (middle of funnel)**, great for **followers**.
- In-depth educational content (step-by-step tutorials)
- Storytelling videos about personal + business wins, losses, experiences

**BOFU (bottom of funnel)**, good for **leads**.
- Personal before-and-after transformations
- Client before-and-after transformations
- "Fake case study" videos, what you'd do for big celebrities / businesses / an average person in XYZ situation
- "What I would do in XYZ situation" videos

## Example
Most creators should run a **mix of all three**, pure TOFU gets views but no leads; pure BOFU gets leads but no audience to convert.

## CTA Per Funnel Stage
Match the CTA to the stage, not just the format:
- **TOFU** → follow CTA, or a CTA that leads to a freebie
- **MOFU** → follow CTA (use a leads CTA if the video is directly about your product/service)
- **BOFU** → ManyChat paid-offer CTA or a lead-magnet CTA

## When to Use
When planning a content calendar: tag each post idea TOFU/MOFU/BOFU and check the format matches the funnel stage. If you want leads, stop posting tier lists.
