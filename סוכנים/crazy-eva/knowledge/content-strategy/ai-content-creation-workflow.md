# AI-Powered Content Creation Workflow

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYj0imohYWv/?igsh=OG02OWJxNWRibjZ6
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-05-21

## Core Insight
Instead of asking AI to just "write content", use AI as a research and repurposing tool, feed it viral data and let it extract patterns, fill-in-the-blank structures, and content ideas from your own real experiences.

## How It Works
- **Hooks**: Feed AI viral outlier hooks from your niche → get written, verbal, and visual hook variations
- **Scripts**: Feed AI multiple viral scripts → have it extract why they went viral → create fill-in-the-blank structures for your niche
- **Carousels**: Take your own viral Reels → have AI convert them to carousels (already proven topics)
- **Content ideas**: Have AI transcribe your calls, review your calendar and emails weekly → surface lesson/experience-based content ideas
- **Stories**: Feed AI your story analytics → identify best performers → create different iterations

## Example
Take a viral script from a different niche → ask AI why it worked → get a fill-in-the-blank structure → fill in with your own niche content.

## When to Use
Weekly content planning sessions; when repurposing existing content; when running out of ideas; when you want to scale content output without losing authenticity.
