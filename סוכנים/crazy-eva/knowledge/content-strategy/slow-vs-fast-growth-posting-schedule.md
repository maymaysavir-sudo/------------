# Slow Growth vs. Fast Growth Posting Schedule

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYuHqwkBCYi/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-05-31

## Core Insight
Your weekly posting volume and format mix determines whether you grow slow or fast. Reels are the growth lever, a fast-growth schedule front-loads multiple Reels per day, while a slow-growth schedule leans on stories + carousels.

## How It Works
Side-by-side weekly comparison (per day):
- **Slow Growth**, ~1 Story + 1 Carousel per day (low reach formats dominate)
- **Fast Growth**, multiple Reels per day + 1 Story + Carousel (e.g. Reel + Story + Reel)
- The difference is the number of Reels: Reels drive new-audience reach; stories/carousels mostly serve existing followers
- Aimed at business owners who want to grow and sell on social media

## When to Use
When deciding a posting cadence for a brand/creator account: if growth is the goal, build the calendar around 1-2+ Reels/day with stories and carousels as support, not the other way around.
