# 8 Ways to Double Down on a Viral Video

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DUx4m8hEs9d/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
When you get a viral video, you have 8 specific ways to double down on it. Each targets a different component of what made it work.

## How It Works
1. **Double down on the visual hook**, recreate the same visual setup/scene that opened the video
2. **Double down on the verbal hook**, reuse the same spoken opening line with new content
3. **Double down on the written hook**, keep the same on-screen text hook format
4. **Double down on all three hooks combined**, keep verbal + written + visual hooks identical, change the meat
5. **Double down on the script structure**, templatize the viral script and reuse the architecture
6. **Double down on the topic**, make another video on the same subject from a different angle
7. **Double down on the format**, reuse the filming/editing format with different content
8. **Refilm and repost**, literally repost the same video without changing anything

## Example
David Sacks style visual hook reuse, Jesha's bagel visual, Ashton's morning routine visual, all creators who found a visual that worked and kept using it.

## When to Use
Immediately after getting a viral video. Don't chase the next new idea, mine the one that already worked. Cycle through all 8 methods before moving on to a new angle.
