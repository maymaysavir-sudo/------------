# Kiss Marry Kill - Content Creation Framework

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYcGJGGBUSp/?igsh=MTgxMjk2Nmx2cjRicw==
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-05-21

## Core Insight
The most important content creation decisions in priority order: idea/topic > written hook > everything else. Format and visual hooks are overrated, if your idea is strong, a basic talking-head video can go viral.

## How It Works
**Hooks:**
- MARRY: Written hook (text on screen), first thing people see, always on
- KISS: Verbal hook, not everyone watches with audio on
- KILL: Visual hook, great for pure virality but attracts low-quality viewers

**Format:**
- KILL: Format, a talking-head video that goes viral means your content is actually good
- MARRY: Idea/topic, strong enough idea works in any format

**Ideation:**
- KISS: Iterating off viral outliers/hooks, proven demand in your niche
- MARRY: Doubling down on YOUR own viral content, audience already validated it
- KILL: Coming up with original ideas, research what works first

**Production:**
- KILL: Editing
- KISS: Scripting
- MARRY: Ideation/research

## Example
If sitting down and talking goes viral, that means your content is so good people don't need production value. Prioritize research over creativity.

## When to Use
When planning content strategy; when deciding where to invest production time; when diagnosing why content isn't performing.
