# Sound Effects 101: A Library for Reels

**Source**: https://www.tiktok.com/@personalbrandlaunch0/video/7649370753695108366
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-06-25

## Core Insight
A categorized library of the sound-effect types that make short-form videos feel polished and retention-friendly. Layering the right SFX is a production lever that lifts a plain talking-head Reel into something more engaging.

## How It Works
Sound effects are grouped into types you can pull from while editing:
- Meme sound effects: instantly-recognizable viral audio stings.
- UI / Tech sound effects: Notification, Mouse Click, Keyboard Typing, Toggle, Glitch, Digital Loading, Glitchy Counter.
- Transition sound effects: whooshes/swipes that cover cuts.
- Cinematic sound effects: Metallic Rise, Boom, Hit, Remembering Woosh, Downer, Heartbeat (build tension/drama).
- Chime sound effects: bright accents to mark a point or reveal.
- Cartoon / Retro sound effects: Pop, Running, Punch, Womp Womp, Blinking, Slide Whistle, Boing (playful, comedic beats).

## Example
On screen the creator labels each waveform with its specific name (e.g. under UI/Tech: Notification Sound, Mouse Click, Keyboard Typing, Glitch; under Cinematic: Metallic Rise, Boom, Heartbeat), giving a vocabulary of effects to grab when editing.

## When to Use
During editing, when a Reel feels flat: pick a SFX category that matches the moment (UI sounds for tech/process clips, cinematic for dramatic reveals, cartoon/retro for comedic punchlines, transitions to smooth cuts) to add polish and hold attention.
