# Learn vs. Don't Learn - Content Edition 2026

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXFTpU3GWAz/?igsh=Z255ZjdicDEzMHZr
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-15

## Core Insight
In 2026, the skills that move the needle are about strategy and substance, not production quality. Stop investing time in fancy editing and equipment; invest it in research, scripting, and conversion.

## How It Works
**Learn:**
- Researching outlier videos for viral hooks, topics, and structures
- Scripting, storytelling, and educational content
- Using engaging formats
- How to get leads from content

**Don't Learn:**
- ADHD editing / fancy editing techniques
- Filming techniques
- Using nice equipment

## Example
A creator who spends 3 hours on transitions vs. one who spends 3 hours researching what hooks are working in their niche, the second one wins every time.

## When to Use
When deciding where to invest time as a creator. Especially useful when feeling stuck on production quality instead of focusing on what actually drives growth and leads.
