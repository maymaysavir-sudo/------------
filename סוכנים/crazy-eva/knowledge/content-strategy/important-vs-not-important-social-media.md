# Important vs. Not Important - Social Media Edition

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DWws2behzma/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
Most creators waste time on things that don't move the needle. Eva's ranking separates what actually grows accounts from what feels productive but doesn't.

## How It Works
**NOT important (stop wasting time):**
- Hashtags
- Reposting new Reels to Stories
- Engaging with others on the platform ("doesn't do a dang thing")
- Doing trends (might get views, not quality followers)
- Trending audios
- Posting at specific times
- Highly produced videos (just need captions)

**IMPORTANT (focus here):**
- One consistent face/person in videos (recognizable personal brand)
- Finding outlier hooks for your videos
- Scripting, never freestyle, especially for storytelling (but don't use AI)
- Making educational content → builds authority and trustworthiness
- Making storytelling content → builds connection and real followers
- Posting consistently
- Using engaging formats
- Editing with captions (90% watch on mute)

## When to Use
When auditing a client's content strategy or your own. If time is limited, cut everything in the "not important" list and double down on the important ones.

## Red Flag / Green Flag Extended (Additional Source)

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DUIsobpjv4C/

**Red flags (stop doing):**
- Hashtags, don't work like they used to (sometimes useful for keywords)
- Using the translation feature, gets you views from people who won't buy
- Posting times, doesn't matter
- Trending audio with long captions, short video + long caption to loop is played out
- Doing trends, gets unengaged followers
- Not using CTAs
- Multiple links in bio, have ONE link only

**Green flags (keep doing):**
- Finding and using outlier hooks
- Doubling down on your content
- Posting educational and storytelling content
- Posting every day
- Using engaging formats
