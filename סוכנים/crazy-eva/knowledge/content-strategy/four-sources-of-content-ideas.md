# The 4 Sources That Keep You Creative (Never Run Out of Video Ideas)

**Added**: 2026-07-20

## Core Insight
Staying creative daily is not about forcing inspiration, it is about running four reliable input streams that continuously feed you ideas: your own testing, reusable script structures, industry observations, and off-platform consumption.

## How It Works
- **1. Experience (your own tests)** - run split tests constantly, for yourself and for clients. Every test produces insights, strategies, and tips that become content. This is the highest-value source because nobody else has your data.
- **2. Formats and script structures** - proven education and value-based script structures circulate constantly (this is how trends actually form). Templatize them into your own niche. Do not make this your sole strategy and avoid fluffy trends, but it is a reliable fallback when you need an idea fast and these formats typically perform.
- **3. Observations** - every industry always has current happenings, news or otherwise. On social specifically, look at what is being posted, what performs and what doesn't, and build content from those observations. Works in any niche.
- **4. Consumption (off-platform)** - research in your niche via podcasts, books, and other authorities in your industry. You get better ideas from touching grass than from scrolling. The loop: learn, test it yourself, then create content on the results.

## Example
The creator started a brand new social page and, despite finding it genuinely hard to come up with ideas for it, has not missed a single day of posting, crediting these four streams for sustaining output across both her own page and long-running client accounts.

## When to Use
When a creator or client is burning out on ideation, hitting the wall on a new page with no existing content bank, or relying entirely on trend-chasing. Pair with the 60-Minute Content Ideas Speed Run: this framework supplies the raw inputs, the sprint converts them into a calendar.
