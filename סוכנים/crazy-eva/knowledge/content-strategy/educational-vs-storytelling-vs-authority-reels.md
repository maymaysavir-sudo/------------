# Educational vs. Storytelling vs. Authority Reels

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DWL5kH_Bqeb/?igsh=MWl4cWhrZzBjbWZ3eQ==
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-03-25

## Core Insight
The 3 Reel types each serve a different purpose in the funnel, Educational builds trust, Storytelling gets followers, Authority gets leads.

## How It Works
- **Educational**, teaches a valuable tip, lesson, or process → goal: build trust → post 3-5x/week → CTA: ManyChat to lead magnet/offer, or follow
- **Storytelling**, shares a win/loss personal or business story → goal: get followers → post 1x/week → CTA: follow
- **Authority**, shows a client or personal before/after transformation → goal: get leads → post 1x/week → CTA: ManyChat to offer or freebie

## Example
Weekly mix: 3-4 educational Reels teaching actionable tips, 1 storytelling Reel sharing a client win story, 1 authority Reel showing a transformation result.

## When to Use
When planning your weekly Reel content mix, use this framework to ensure every Reel has a clear purpose and the right CTA for that purpose.
