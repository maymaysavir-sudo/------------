# 0 to 10,000 Followers: The 7-Step Growth System

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbIo3i_vwp1/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-07-24

## Core Insight
Growing an account from 0 to 10,000 followers isn't luck or volume-for-volume's-sake; it's a repeatable 7-step pipeline you run on a 2-week-to-1-month cycle: define the niche, fix the profile, set a cadence, research proven outliers, ideate from that research, script from your own experience, then film/edit/upload, and repeat until you hit the goal. It stitches together the individual crazy-eva frameworks into one ordered system.

## How It Works
1. **Determine account niche + sub-topics.** Write your (a) niche/broad industry, (b) sub-topics you'll make content around, (c) your occupation/title. These feed a keyword list you'll search later. (See [1-Year Content Multiplication Formula](./1-year-content-multiplication-formula.md).)
2. **Optimize your profile.** Fix link in bio, username, tagline, profile picture, and bio (wrong vs. right versions). (See [Profile Audit in 5 Minutes](./profile-audit-5-minutes.md).)
3. **Set posting amount + schedule.** Pick an intensity (easy / moderate / hard). Which days you post barely matters; the schedule exists to keep you accountable.
4. **Research (never throw spaghetti at the wall).** Find proven viral outlier hooks + topics. Measure with the **5x rule**: outliers that got ~5x the creator's follower count in views. Two methods: (a) keyword research, search each keyword from step 1 on Explore and save 5x-rule videos; (b) then scroll the Explore "recommended" tab, which now surfaces newer, recently-viral videos around those topics. End with a stash of saved 5x-rule hooks in your niche. (See [The 5x Rule](./the-5x-rule-competitor-research.md) and [Explore Research Method](./viral-content-research-explore-method.md).)
5. **Ideation (the most important part).** Build **hook stacks**: pull viral written/verbal/visual hooks from your saved outliers, get ~3 options per script. Then choose the script's value (which of *your* experiences/knowledge fuels it), choose a script structure/template (from educational + storytelling viral videos), and choose the filming format that keeps it engaging.
6. **Script your ideations.** Write out each script, making sure the value comes from your own knowledge/experience (not generic regurgitation).
7. **Film, edit, upload, and grow.** Batch everything for the next 2 weeks to 1 month so it's ready to go, then repeat the whole cycle until you reach 10,000 followers.

## Example
The creator frames it as "if I wanted to grow my Instagram from 0 to 10k, this is how I'd do it," then batches 2 weeks-to-1-month of scripts at a time so content is always ready, repeating the loop until the follower goal is hit. (Done-for-you offer: "comment SMM.")

## When to Use
When you (or a client) are starting or restarting an account and want a single ordered playbook from zero, rather than picking individual tactics. Use it as the spine, and drill into the linked granular frameworks for each step.
