# Six-Pillar Script Writing Method

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZ8KY50p4JU/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-06-25

## Core Insight
A repeatable six-pillar framework turns a flat, low-view video into a high-performing one by forcing you to deliberately choose each script element instead of winging it: Topic, Hook, Structure, Format, Value, and CTA.

## How It Works
- Pillar 1 - Topic: a niche is made of many sub-niches. Pick ONE sub-niche as the topic of this specific video (e.g. fitness breaks into cardio, lifting, nutrition, protein, sleep).
- Pillar 2 - Hook: the first 3 seconds. Go to Instagram Explore, search your video's topic, open the Reels tab, and scroll until you find a video that follows the "5X rule" (5x more views than that creator's follower count). Reverse-engineer its written, verbal, and visual hook.
- Pillar 3 - Structure: there are multiple ways to structure a video. Deliberately pick one for the script.
- Pillar 4 - Format: there are multiple filming formats. Deliberately pick one.
- Pillar 5 - Value: use your own knowledge/experience to write the actual value, plus any editing notes and filming instructions.
- Pillar 6 - CTA: two main CTA types: follows or leads. Reuse proven templates for whichever you want.

## Example
Demonstrated with a "157 views vs 1M views" split-screen: the same topic flops with no script and goes viral once run through the six pillars. Niche-to-sub-niche example shown: FITNESS -> Cardio, Lifting/Building Muscle, Nutrition, Protein, Sleep.

## When to Use
When you (or a client) sit down to write a short-form script and don't know where to start, or when videos are underperforming because they were posted without a deliberate hook, structure, and CTA.
