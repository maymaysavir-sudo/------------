# The 3 Types of Instagram Research That Grow Your Reels

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZzpJojhoIy/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-06-22

## Core Insight
To grow your Reels and following you need three distinct types of research: creator research, keyword research, and Reels recommendation / explore page research. Each surfaces virally proven hooks and topics that you remix with your own value and stories so content stays authentic but proven.

## How It Works
- Creator research: find viral outlier videos from small-to-mid creators in your niche by searching your topic in Instagram and going to the Accounts and Reels tabs. Batch it about once a month
- Keyword research: search your niche or a topic in the Instagram search tab and the top videos pop up. Save the ones that follow the 5X rule with hooks you like. Batch it once or twice a month
- Reels recommendation tab and explore page research: after creator and keyword research on a topic, your explore page starts serving outlier videos on that niche, showing what is recently working. Do this a few times a week
- Use the viral videos by lifting their proven hooks and topics, then layer in your own value, experience and stories so it stays authentic but virally proven
- Match research type to need: keyword research for written and verbal hooks, Reels tab and explore page for visual hooks and formats, creator or keyword research for topics

## Example
She demonstrates with the dog-training niche: searching it on Instagram, filtering by Accounts and Reels to find dog trainers, then showing the explore page filling with dog-training outlier Reels after repeated research.

## When to Use
When you want a repeatable system for sourcing proven, on-trend Reel ideas and hooks instead of guessing what to post. Related: [[viral-content-research-explore-method]], [[the-5x-rule-competitor-research]].
