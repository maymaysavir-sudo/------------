# What to Do After a Viral Video - 7-Component Template

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DWyiV2IDwFN/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
When you get a viral video, don't just keep posting normally. Break it down into 7 components, templatize it, and repeat it with slightly adjusted value until it stops performing.

## How It Works
Break the viral video into these 7 components:
1. **Verbal hook**, what was said in the first line
2. **Written hook**, the on-screen text hook
3. **Visual hook**, the visual setup/scene that opened the video
4. **Structure**, the script architecture (how the video was built)
5. **Format**, how it was filmed (talking head, voiceover, green screen, etc.)
6. **Editing**, cuts, pacing, captions, effects used
7. **Topic + Value**, what subject it covered and what the viewer got from it
8. **CTA**, what action it asked the viewer to take

Then: templatize the whole thing into a repeatable template → adjust the value slightly → recreate → repeat until it stops performing.

## Example
If a "3 mistakes new coaches make" video goes viral: keep the written hook format, same visual setup, same structure, but next time make it "3 mistakes new freelancers make."

## When to Use
Every time a client or your own account gets a viral video. This is the highest-ROI content move available, stop searching for the next idea and mine what already worked.
