# Sound Effects 101 - 7 Types for Video Engagement

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DWOe5DnjLHF/?igsh=MmZhM2I3MGVvdWVl
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-03-25

## Core Insight
Adding sound effects to videos makes them significantly more engaging. There are 7 categories to choose from depending on your content tone and style.

## How It Works
1. **Meme / Comedy**, Vine-coded sounds, great for humor and relatability
2. **UI / Tech**, computer and iPhone action sounds
3. **Transition**, sounds that punctuate cuts and movement between scenes
4. **Cinematic**, dramatic, high-production feel
5. **Action**, physical action sound effects
6. **Cartoon**, exaggerated cartoon/character sounds
7. **Gaming**, game-inspired sound effects

## Example
A talking-head Reel about productivity could use UI/tech sounds when referencing apps, transition sounds between points, and a meme sound for a punchline moment.

## When to Use
During video editing, pick a sound effect category that matches your content's tone to add a layer of engagement beyond music and voiceover.
