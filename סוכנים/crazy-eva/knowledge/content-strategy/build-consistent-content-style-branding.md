# Build a Consistent Content Style: Colors, Fonts, Visuals

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZsthAIMgG2/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-06-23

## Core Insight
"Perfect branding" creators are not more talented, they just locked in three repeatable choices: colors, fonts, and visual types. Decide them once and reuse them on every video, and your content instantly looks consistent and recognizable.

## How It Works
- Step 1, Colors: pick two to three colors using one color rule. Options shown: 60/30/10 rule, golden ratio, complementary colors, analogous colors, or triadic colors. Pick one rule, get your palette, done.
- Step 2, Fonts: pick two to three. A primary font for written hooks and the bold, big text in the video. A secondary font for regular captions. An optional accent font used sparingly for little annotations (example shown: Inter as a primary font).
- Step 3, Visuals: choose two to three consistent visual types you reuse in every video. Options: screenshots, screen recordings, b-roll, images and GIFs, charts and graphs.
- The payoff: colors and fonts you only choose once, then use those same choices to edit every video from then on. That repetition is what reads as "branding."

## Example
She breaks down a real creator's setup on screen: the color rules board (60/30/10, golden ratio, analogous, triadic, complementary) with example posts under each; a primary/secondary/accent font diagram using @chloe.shih's "When is it time to walk away from a friend" post; and a visuals board labeling charts/graphs, images, and b-roll.

## When to Use
When your feed looks random or off-brand, or when starting a new account. Set the three pillars once (colors, fonts, visual types) and apply them to every edit so the account looks intentional and cohesive.
