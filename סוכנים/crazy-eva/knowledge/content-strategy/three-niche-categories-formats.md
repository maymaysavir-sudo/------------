# Three Niche Categories - Match Formats to Visual / Verbal / Product

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXmxe9Jh9IE/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-27

## Core Insight
Every social niche falls into one of three categories, visual, verbal, or product, and each category has its own video structures and formats that outperform. Stop copy-pasting formats across niches; match the format to how your audience actually learns the thing.

## How It Works

**Category 1, Visual niches (you see to learn)**
- Structures that work: tutorials, transformations, tips/hacks
- Formats that work: voiceover, shot/angle changes, pure visuals

**Category 2, Verbal niches (you hear to learn)**
- Structures that work: tutorials, tips/hacks, right-vs-wrong / correct-vs-incorrect
- Formats that work: clones, talking back-and-forth, green screen, whiteboard, reactions

**Category 3, Product niches (you see and hear to learn)**
- Structures that work: comparisons, before-and-after, storytelling
- Formats that work: voiceovers, visuals, clones, reactions

## Example
Hairstyling = visual niche → transformation videos with shot changes. Mindset coaching = verbal niche → green-screen rants or clone debates. Software/apps = product niche → before-and-after demos with voiceover.

## When to Use
Before scripting a new piece of content, diagnose your niche category first, then pick from the matched structure × format menu instead of mimicking a creator from a different category.
