# Video vs. Script - Behind-the-Scenes Content Format

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DWrDHg2jeyO/?igsh=MTA0amVhcjdndW9qdQ==
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-19

## Core Insight
Showing "this is my video / this is my script" side-by-side is a repeatable content format that generates leads (viewers comment "SCRIPT" to receive scripts) while also demonstrating authority.

## How It Works
- Film or screenshot your video alongside the script used to create it
- Show multiple examples in one reel (4 pairs in this case)
- Close with a comment CTA: "Comment SCRIPT and I'll send them over"
- Works as a lead magnet + credibility proof in one piece of content

## Example
Eva showed 4 video+script pairs in one reel with a single CTA at the end.

## When to Use
When you want to build an email/DM list, demonstrate your scripting process, or repurpose existing viral content as a new piece of authority content.
