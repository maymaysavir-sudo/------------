# Instagram vs. TikTok - Content Strategy Differences

**Source**: https://vt.tiktok.com/ZS9XLjehy/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-05-05

## Core Insight
Instagram and TikTok reward fundamentally different content approaches, scripted vs. unscripted, quality vs. quantity, aesthetic vs. raw. Trying to post the same content on both platforms underperforms on each.

## How It Works
| Factor | Instagram | TikTok |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Format | Scripted | Unscripted |
| Content type | Educational + storytelling | Entertaining + storytelling |
| Editing style | Aesthetic, polished, well-produced | Simple white text + cuts |
| Audience age | Older + younger | Younger |
| Posting cadence | Quality: 3-4x per week | Quantity: 1-3x per day |
| Key metrics | Watch time, shares, saves | Retention, shares |

## Example
The same tutorial topic posted as a polished, scripted reel on Instagram vs. a raw, off-the-cuff TikTok with text-on-screen cuts, the TikTok version should lean casual and high-frequency, the Instagram version should be more produced and intentional.

## When to Use
When planning a dual-platform content strategy or deciding how to adapt a piece of content for a second platform. Do not simply repost, adjust format and production level to match platform expectations.
