# Account-Ruining Behaviors - Tier List

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DUsvisWjvF_/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-04-07

## Core Insight
Not all account mistakes are equally damaging. Eva rates them by severity so you know what to never do vs. what's just mildly bad.

## How It Works
- **S tier (worst)**, Buying bot followers / fake engagement: seen it ruin big and small accounts; growth stops immediately. Do not do this.
- **S tier**, Inconsistent posting / long breaks: she passed many "top dogs" in her niche purely because they took breaks and she never did. Coming back after a long break is always painful.
- **B tier**, Getting shadow banned: hurts your account for months or up to a year, but eventually resolves on its own.
- **C tier**, Using the translation feature: attracts non-buyer followers, which tanks your engagement ratio and hurts future reach.
- **D tier**, Deleting bad performing posts: not catastrophic, but they sometimes pop off later. Put your ego away and leave them up.
- **Low tier**, Posting videos with watermarks: only hurts that individual post, not the overall account.

## When to Use
When a client or creator asks "is it okay if I…", use this as a quick reference. The S-tier items are absolute no-gos; everything else is judgment calls.
