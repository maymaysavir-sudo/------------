# Script Structures 101 - Templatize Viral, Repost in Your Niche

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYZhUANBb7Z/
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-05-17

## Core Insight
Every week / month / year on social media there are script structures going viral. The play: spot them, templatize them, adapt to your niche, and post, repeat indefinitely. The structures are predictable; the topic and voice you bring on top are not.

## How It Works
- Spot the viral script as it surfaces (across niches, doesn't have to be yours)
- Extract the structural skeleton (hook → setup → payoff sequence, not the words)
- Templatize it: fill-in-the-blanks version that works for any topic
- Adapt to your niche and voice
- Post and measure
- Eva practices what she preaches, posts the structures she recommends and uses results as social proof
- The same loop runs continuously: there's always another structure going viral

## Example
"This was a viral script structure going around. I templatized it, did it for my niche, and threw up the results.", that exact frame is itself the templatized structure, repeated 4× in the video as the demo.

## When to Use
As the standing weekly workflow for any content account that wants to ride trends without inventing from scratch. Builds a personal library of templatized structures over time, that library compounds.
